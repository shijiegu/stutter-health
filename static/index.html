stutter-health

Baseline shell for an ultra-low-latency accessibility client. Audio on the wire is raw PCM 16-bit little-endian at 24 kHz.

Speech-to-text

Connect to /ws/stt and stream microphone frames as binary PCM. Transcript events from Cartesia Ink-2 come back as JSON.

Text-to-speech

Connect to /ws/tts, send JSON such as {"text":"Hello"}, and play the binary PCM chunks as they arrive from Sonic.
